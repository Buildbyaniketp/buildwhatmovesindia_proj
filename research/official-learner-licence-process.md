# Official learner's licence application journey: India baseline and West Bengal implementation

**Research date:** 23 August 2026

**Scope:** First-time learner's licence (LL), with West Bengal as the implementation reference.
**Source rule:** Only first-party Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Parivahan/Sarathi, and West Bengal Transport Department sources were used. The supplied PDF was inspected only for labels and layout; no entered personal data is reproduced here.

## Executive answer

The safest product model is not a single universal Indian wizard. The legal core is common - an application to the licensing authority, eligibility and document evidence, the prescribed fee, a preliminary knowledge test unless exempt, and an issue/refuse decision - but Parivahan itself says the online flow may differ by state. The current West Bengal Sarathi implementation exposes a seven-stage applicant sequence: **fill LL application details, upload documents, upload photo and signature, pay the fee, verify payment, print the receipt, and book the LL slot**. Aadhaar eKYC changes both uploads and office attendance; test and printing facilities are also state-dependent. [West Bengal Transport Department, "Learner"](https://transport.wb.gov.in/governance-services/driving-license/learner/) [Central CMV Form 2, pp. 1-5](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf) [Parivahan, "How to apply for Learner's License" (flow may differ by state)](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan//en/content/how-apply-learners-license)

For the current West Bengal portal, the applicant-category choices are exactly **Diplomats (Foreigner), Foreigners (But not Diplomats), General, OCI, Divyang(Differently Abled), Repatriate, Refugees, and Ex-Servicemen**. The control is labelled **"Select Category if required"**. It does **not** offer OBC or Other. "Others" is instead a class-of-vehicle option in statutory Form 2. [West Bengal Sarathi, applicant-category screen](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_displayNewLL.do) [Central CMV Form 2, p. 1](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf)

## 1. Exact macro journey

### A. Start and choose a path

1. Open Sarathi, choose the state from which the service is to be taken, then choose **Apply for Learner Licence**. The state choice is fundamental because only listed states expose online services and the subsequent implementation is state-owned. [Sarathi state selection](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/stateSelection.do) [West Bengal Sarathi home](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/stateSelectBean.do)
2. In West Bengal, Sarathi first offers a contactless Aadhaar/eKYC path or a **Skip** route. The portal states that contactless service depends on Aadhaar details and an Aadhaar-linked mobile; the new-LL authentication screen separately offers **Submit via Aadhaar Authentication** and **Submit without Aadhaar Authentication**. [West Bengal Sarathi new-LL entry](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLLDet.do) [West Bengal Sarathi authentication screen](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_execute.do)
3. Review the official seven stages shown before application entry: **(1) Fill application details LL, (2) Upload documents, (3) Upload photo and signature, (4) Fee payment, (5) Verify the payment status, (6) Print the receipt, (7) LL slot book**. With Aadhaar eKYC, the screen says only the signature needs upload rather than both photo and signature. [West Bengal Transport Department, stages 1-7](https://transport.wb.gov.in/governance-services/driving-license/learner/) [West Bengal Sarathi new-LL instructions](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLLDet.do)

### B. Establish applicant status before entering the long form

4. Choose an applicant category if required, then state whether the applicant **does not hold any Driving/Learner licence issued in India**, **holds Driving Licence**, or **holds Learner Licence**. These choices determine whether existing licence details are required. [West Bengal Sarathi applicant-category screen](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_displayNewLL.do) [Central CMV Form 2, p. 3, "Particulars of existing Licence"](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf)
5. Choose Aadhaar or non-Aadhaar authentication. In the current West Bengal screen, Aadhaar-authenticated applicants may take the LL test from home or another preferred location without visiting the RTO, receive the test password by SMS to the Aadhaar-linked mobile, and may download the LL after success. A non-Aadhaar applicant must visit the RTO for document verification, although the same screen says the LL test itself may still be taken from home or another preferred location. The non-Aadhaar branch then asks for a mobile number, CAPTCHA, and OTP generation. [West Bengal Sarathi authentication screen](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_execute.do)

### C. Complete application, evidence, and payment

6. Fill applicant, relationship, birth, contact, address, class-of-vehicle, physical-fitness declaration, and final declaration fields; select the RTO office and requested vehicle class(es). The current West Bengal form also asks whether training was received from a driving school and requires Form 1 details before submission. [Provided West Bengal Sarathi capture, pp. 1-3](</Users/aniketpaul/Desktop/Application For NewLL.pdf>) [Central CMV Form 2, pp. 1-3](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf)
7. Upload the required proofs. Statutory Form 2 permits one proof common to age and address from a list that includes Aadhaar, electoral roll, life-insurance policy, passport, school certificate, birth certificate, a government/local-body payslip, specified affidavit/certificate routes, or another state-specified document. Additional documents apply to specific cases. [Central CMV Form 2, p. 5](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf)
8. Upload photo and signature, except that the West Bengal eKYC path says only a signature upload is needed. Then pay the application/test fee. [West Bengal Transport Department, stages 3-4](https://transport.wb.gov.in/governance-services/driving-license/learner/)
9. Verify payment status and print the payment receipt as separate steps. Sarathi's payment help says payment uses application number and date of birth, includes fee calculation, payment mode/gateway and CAPTCHA, generates a Sarathi Token Number for reference, and may generate a new token after a failed transaction. Verification again requires application number, date of birth and CAPTCHA; receipt printing requires application number and CAPTCHA. [Sarathi payment instructions](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/paymentscov/HELP.htm)

### D. Slot, preliminary test, and LL output

10. Book the LL test slot where the state/RTO flow requires one. West Bengal explicitly places **LL SLOT BOOK** after printing the receipt. Its booking entry asks for application number, applicant date of birth and CAPTCHA, and explains that the application number is the one issued at application submission. [West Bengal Transport Department, stage 7](https://transport.wb.gov.in/governance-services/driving-license/learner/) [Sarathi LL test slot booking](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/slots/llslotbook.do?id=sarlltest)
11. Complete the road-safety tutorial and preliminary knowledge test. For states that expose the online STALL test, the official route is Sarathi -> state -> **Online Learners' License Test (STALL)** -> enter LL application, date of birth and password -> log in. Parivahan explicitly says STALL is available only in some states; the current West Bengal authentication screen is the stronger state-specific evidence for at-home testing in this POC. [Parivahan, online LL test steps](https://www.parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/en/content/what-will-be-steps-online-learning-license-test) [West Bengal Sarathi authentication screen](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_execute.do)
12. The licensing authority records the preliminary test as pass, fail, absent, or exempted and issues or refuses the LL. After an approved pass, the applicant may use **Learner License -> Print Learner License (Form 3)** with application number and date of birth where the state permits applicant printing; the national FAQ says this print service is state-dependent. The current West Bengal authentication page says successful applicants can download their LL. [Central CMV Form 2, p. 4](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf) [Parivahan, print Learner License (Form 3)](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/en/content/i-am-unable-print-learner-license) [Official Parivahan FAQ, print availability varies by state](https://staging.parivahan.gov.in/hi/content/faq)
13. If absent or failed, pay the retest fee and book a fresh appointment through **Appointments -> Slot booking Learner's License Test**. [Parivahan, absent/failed LL test](https://parivahan.gov.in/en/node/70)

## 4. Process friction evidenced by the official flow

These are **observable process/interaction burdens**, not claims from user research.

- **Fragmented completion:** West Bengal presents seven separately named stages, including a separate payment-status verification after payment and a separate receipt-print step before slot booking. [West Bengal Transport Department](https://transport.wb.gov.in/governance-services/driving-license/learner/)
- **Repeated identifiers and CAPTCHAs:** payment, verification, and receipt printing each ask again for identifiers; payment and verification use application number/date of birth/CAPTCHA, while receipt printing uses application number/CAPTCHA. [Sarathi payment instructions](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/paymentscov/HELP.htm)
- **Payment recovery overhead:** Sarathi generates a token for reference and states that a failed transaction starts a new payment process with a new token. [Sarathi payment instructions](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/paymentscov/HELP.htm)
- **Aadhaar dependencies:** the contactless path requires matching Aadhaar details and an Aadhaar-linked mobile; the test password is sent to that linked mobile. The non-Aadhaar path adds mobile, CAPTCHA and OTP plus an RTO document-verification visit. [West Bengal Sarathi authentication](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_execute.do)
- **Official-source inconsistency:** the older West Bengal information page lists Aadhaar Card among "Necessary Documents," but statutory Form 2 offers multiple age/address proofs and the current West Bengal portal explicitly supports submission without Aadhaar. A prototype should not present Aadhaar as universally mandatory. [West Bengal Transport Department](https://transport.wb.gov.in/governance-services/driving-license/learner/) [Central CMV Form 2, p. 5](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf) [West Bengal Sarathi authentication](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_execute.do)
- **Category ambiguity:** the control says "Select Category if required" but provides no explanation on that screen of who must select which status; it also distinguishes two foreigner categories plus OCI, repatriate and refugee. [West Bengal Sarathi applicant-category screen](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_displayNewLL.do)
- **Existing-licence formatting trap:** the live West Bengal start screen instructs applicants to enter existing LL/DL numbers exactly, including spaces and slashes, and disables copy/paste in those fields. [West Bengal Sarathi applicant-category/status screen](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_displayNewLL.do)
- **Dense address entry:** the form repeats hierarchical present/permanent address controls and adds duration of stay, although it provides a same-as-present shortcut. [Provided West Bengal capture, pp. 1-2](</Users/aniketpaul/Desktop/Application For NewLL.pdf>)
- **Name constraint:** the West Bengal form warns that a middle name longer than six characters should be entered in the first-name or last-name field for printing. [Provided West Bengal capture, p. 1](</Users/aniketpaul/Desktop/Application For NewLL.pdf>)
- **Desktop-specific multi-select:** the COV control instructs the applicant to hold the Ctrl key to select multiple vehicle classes. [Provided West Bengal capture, p. 2](</Users/aniketpaul/Desktop/Application For NewLL.pdf>)
- **Conditional evidence is scattered:** minor consent, foreigner legal-presence proof, transport/adapted-vehicle medical evidence, driving-school evidence, and photo requirements sit in the Form 2 annexure rather than in a single universal checklist. [Central CMV Form 2, p. 5](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf) [MoRTH G.S.R. 240(E), pp. 35-36](https://morth.nic.in/sites/default/files/notifications_document/GSR%20240%28E%29%20dated%2031st%20March%202021%20Committee%20A%20Licensing%20of%20drivers%2C%20Fitness%20and%20Registration%20of%20motor%20vehicles%20%20.pdf)
- **Failure loops:** an absent or failed test requires both a retest-fee transaction and a fresh appointment. [Parivahan retest FAQ](https://parivahan.gov.in/en/node/70)
- **Online-test gates:** STALL uses application number/CAPTCHA followed by date of birth and a six-character password, provides a separate password-resend route, and makes the road-safety tutorial mandatory before the test. [West Bengal Sarathi STALL authentication](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/authenticationaction.do)
- **State uncertainty:** STALL and Form 3 applicant printing are expressly state-dependent, so a national-looking UI cannot honestly promise either without a state capability check. [Parivahan STALL FAQ](https://www.parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/en/content/what-will-be-steps-online-learning-license-test) [Official Parivahan FAQ](https://staging.parivahan.gov.in/hi/content/faq)

## 5. Honest five-day POC boundary

### The POC can credibly simulate

- A West Bengal-first **journey overview** with the official stages and clear not-started/in-progress/complete/blocked states.
- State, exact applicant category, existing-licence status, Aadhaar/non-Aadhaar, age and COV branching.
- Progressive Form 2-inspired fields populated only with fictional test data.
- A conditional document-readiness checklist for minor, foreigner, transport/adapted COV, eKYC and non-eKYC cases.
- Local/mock uploads for documents, photo and signature without transmitting them.
- Mock fee calculation, payment result, verification state, receipt, slot inventory and booking confirmation, all prominently labelled as demonstrations.
- A short road-safety tutorial, representative mock quiz, pass/fail/retry state and a sample non-official LL/receipt output.
- Resume, correction, dependency explanation, and handoff links to the official Sarathi route.

These simulations mirror the official branch points and stages but do not claim integration with Sarathi, UIDAI, an RTO, a bank, or the licensing authority. The official flow requires live authentication, documents, payment, appointment/test systems and an authority decision. [West Bengal Transport Department](https://transport.wb.gov.in/governance-services/driving-license/learner/) [Central CMV Form 2, pp. 4-5](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf)

### The POC must hand off

- Aadhaar authentication/eKYC, OTP delivery or validation, and any demographic match.
- CAPTCHA solving or validation.
- Real personal-data/document collection, storage, validation, e-signature, photo/biometric capture or medical certification.
- Real fee calculation, payment gateway, token, payment verification, refund/reconciliation or official receipt generation.
- Real RTO/slot availability, booking, cancellation or rescheduling.
- The official STALL or RTO knowledge test, tutorial completion tracking, proctoring, pass/fail decision or exemption.
- RTO document verification and any licensing-authority approval/refusal.
- Official Form 3 generation, LL download/printing, application-status updates and official notifications.
- Legal eligibility determinations or guarantees that a document/category will be accepted.

The handoff should preserve only non-sensitive progress context and tell the applicant exactly which official stage comes next; it should never visually imitate a completed government transaction. This boundary follows from the official sequence's reliance on Aadhaar/mobile authentication, payment/CAPTCHA, appointment/test services and the licensing authority's recorded decision. [West Bengal Sarathi authentication](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_execute.do) [Sarathi payment instructions](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/paymentscov/HELP.htm) [Central CMV Form 2, p. 4](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/sites/default/files/DownloadForm/cmvr/FORM-2.pdf)

## 6. Save, resume, and citizen-account model

**Follow-up verification date:** 24 August 2026.

### Finding

The statement "there is no way to save" is too broad. The narrower verified statement is: **if an LL/DL application session expires before submission, that session's data is not saved and the applicant must apply again**. No Save/Save draft control was visible on the West Bengal new-LL instructions or category/path screens, and the supplied personal-details capture ends with Submit, Refresh and Cancel rather than Save. Because this audit did not enter personal data or submit the form, it did not test any hidden autosave behaviour; the official session-expiry FAQ is the decisive evidence about recoverability. [Parivahan FAQ, "Session expired while applying for Learner's Licence or Driving Licence"](https://parivahan.gov.in/hi/node/100) [West Bengal Sarathi new-LL instructions](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLLDet.do) [West Bengal category/path screen](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/newLL_displayNewLL.do) [provided West Bengal form capture, p. 3](</Users/aniketpaul/Desktop/Application For NewLL.pdf>)

After the application is submitted, however, Sarathi creates an application record and later stages are technically continuable. The public flow does this through an application number and separate stage-specific services, not through restoration of the earlier browser session. MoRTH/NIC describes Sarathi 4.0 as generating a unique application number for each filed application so that the citizen can track its status. [MoRTH/NIC *Next Mile*, April 2016, p. 16](https://www.parivahan.gov.in/nextmile/system/files/pdf/April%202016.pdf)

### Not a demonstrated citizen progress profile

The current public Sarathi state-selection page exposes a generic **Login** link, but it opens a NIC CAS sign-in asking for username, password and CAPTCHA and shows no Create account/Register/citizen-profile option. It should not be presented as a citizen account merely because it is labelled Login. The separately named **Dashboard** links to Sarathi reporting with state/RTO filters and aggregate licence, application and revenue statistics rather than an individual's progress. Therefore, no citizen account/profile/progress dashboard was found in the inspected West Bengal public LL flow; the evidence supports **identifier-based continuation**. [Sarathi state-selection page](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/stateSelection.do) [Sarathi CAS sign-in](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/sarathilogin.do) [Sarathi public reporting dashboard](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/SarathiReport) [Parivahan public analytics dashboard](https://analytics.parivahan.gov.in/analytics/)

One official document uses broader language: the portal privacy policy refers to registration, a "registered user," and a right to update a profile. That policy covers the Parivahan Portal/App generally and does not document a West Bengal new-LL citizen dashboard or change the identifier-based continuation observed in the LL flow. It should be treated as a caveat, not as evidence that this specific journey has a user-facing account. [Sarathi/Parivahan privacy policy, p. 1](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/DrafteTransportPrivacyPolicyv4_12.12.2022.pdf)

### How continuation works after submission

| Later task | Public continuation route and credentials | What this demonstrates | Official evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| See outstanding work / application status | West Bengal exposes **Complete your Pending Application** and **Application Status**, routing to the application-status module; its entry uses application number, date of birth and CAPTCHA. | A submitted record can be reopened by identifiers; this is not restoration of an unsubmitted draft. | [West Bengal Sarathi home](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/stateSelectBean.do); [application-status module](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/applViewStatus.do) |
| Reprint forms, appointment or acknowledgement | Open **Application Status**, enter application number and date of birth, then use the forms/appointment/acknowledgement links shown with the application. | Outputs are retrieved from the submitted application record. | [Official Parivahan FAQ](https://staging.parivahan.gov.in/hi/content/faq) |
| Edit a submitted LL application | Choose **Application Edit (Learners License Only)** from the Learner Licence menu, provide the application details, and later attend the RTO with originals. | Editing is a separate post-application service, not a draft-save control in the initial form. | [Official Parivahan FAQ](https://staging.parivahan.gov.in/hi/content/faq) |
| Upload/re-upload documents | Choose the separate **Upload Document** service after selecting the state; availability depends on state customisation. | Document completion is a module reached after an application exists. | [Parivahan document-upload FAQ](https://parivahan.gov.in/hi/node/162); [West Bengal Sarathi upload module](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/uploadMain.do) |
| Pay or verify payment | Application fee and payment-status verification require application number, date of birth and CAPTCHA; receipt printing requires application number and CAPTCHA. | Payment, verification and receipt are separate identifier-gated modules. | [Sarathi payment instructions](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/paymentscov/HELP.htm) |
| Book the LL test slot | The West Bengal LL slot entry uses application number, applicant date of birth and CAPTCHA. | Booking resumes from the submitted record, not a profile dashboard. | [Sarathi LL test slot booking](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/slots/llslotbook.do?id=sarlltest) |
| Take the online STALL test | Enter LL application number, date of birth and the issued test password. | Test access is another credentialed module and is state-dependent. | [Parivahan STALL instructions](https://www.parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/en/content/what-will-be-steps-online-learning-license-test) |
| Print the issued LL | Use **Print Learner License (Form 3)** with application number and date of birth where the state enables applicant printing. | The issued output is retrieved by application identifiers. | [Parivahan Form 3 print instructions](https://parivahan.gov.in/parivahan/en/content/i-am-unable-print-learner-license) |
| Recover a lost application number | Select the state, then **Others -> Find Application Number**. | Recovery exists, but it is a separate lookup route rather than account history. | [Official Parivahan FAQ](https://staging.parivahan.gov.in/hi/content/faq) |

The West Bengal public home exposes these as separate tiles or menu modules - **Fee Payments, Appointments, Check Payment Status, Upload Document, Print Application Forms, Complete your Pending Application**, and **Application Status** - rather than as tasks inside one citizen workspace. Both **Complete your Pending Application** and **Application Status** resolve to the same `applViewStatus.do` route. [West Bengal Sarathi home](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/stateSelectBean.do) [West Bengal application-status module](https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/sarathiservice/applViewStatus.do)

**Product implication:** Licence Path's Journey Passport may honestly provide a cohesive fictional save/resume experience because it is an independent companion, but it must not describe that experience as mirroring a Sarathi citizen account. The official handoff should say that an unsubmitted Sarathi form is not recoverable after session expiry, while a submitted application continues through its application number and the relevant official module.
